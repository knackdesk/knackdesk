# Renewal Commission Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of independent insurance agencies and brokerages, and individual producers.
**Problem:** Agency owners and producers cannot easily see what their book pays in renewal commission over several years once policy losses compound.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: annual book commission = policies × premium × renewal % ÷ 100; first-year renewal = book × retention % ÷ 100; residuals = Σ n=1..years of book × (retention % ÷ 100)^n; policies remaining = policies × (retention % ÷ 100)^years; new business commission = policies × premium × new business % ÷ 100. Policies of 0, any percentage above 100 and years below 1 or not whole are rejected. Page explains compounding retention and the no-premium-change assumption.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical retention rates, typical commission percentages, valuation multiples, averages or ranges, licensing, carrier appointments, compliance, advice on what to sell, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
