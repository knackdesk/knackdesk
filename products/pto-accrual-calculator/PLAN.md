# PTO Accrual Calculator

**Audience:** small employers, managers and employees checking pay figures.
**Problem:** Nobody knows how many days they have actually earned by a given date.
**Monetization:** AdSense + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: perPeriod = annualDays ÷ periodsPerYear; accruedToDate = perPeriod × periodsElapsed; balance = carriedOver + accruedToDate − taken; periodsPerYear: weekly 52, biweekly 26, semimonthly 24, monthly 12.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, employment-law, tax or benefits advice (rates and rules vary; the tool applies the numbers you enter).
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
