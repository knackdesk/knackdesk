# Job Quote Calculator

**Audience:** contractors, tradespeople and service businesses quoting jobs.
**Problem:** Quotes built from cost plus a guess leave out overhead and confuse markup with margin.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell (Agency Quote & Project Profit Tracker, Freelance Pricing Kit).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: direct cost = materials + hours × rate + subcontractors; overhead = direct × overhead %; total cost = direct + overhead; price = total cost ÷ (1 − margin %); profit = price − total cost; markup = profit ÷ total cost × 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, regional rate tables, tax, contract or legal advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
