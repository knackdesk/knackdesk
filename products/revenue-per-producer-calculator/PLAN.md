# Revenue per Producer Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of independent insurance agencies and brokerages, and individual producers.
**Problem:** Agency owners want to track productivity per producer and per head, and how much revenue goes back to producers, without a spreadsheet.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: revenue per producer = revenue ÷ producers; revenue per employee = revenue ÷ (producers + support); compensation ratio % = compensation ÷ revenue × 100 (0 when revenue is 0); new business share % = new business ÷ revenue × 100 (0 when revenue is 0); new business per producer = new business ÷ producers. Producers of 0 and new business above revenue are rejected. Page says to compare against the agency's own earlier periods.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical retention rates, typical commission percentages, valuation multiples, averages or ranges, licensing, carrier appointments, compliance, advice on what to sell, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
