# Prime Cost Calculator

**Audience:** restaurant, bar and cafe owners and managers reviewing a week or month.
**Problem:** The two biggest controllable costs are often reviewed separately, so nobody sees what they take out of sales together.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: prime cost = food cost + beverage cost + labour cost; prime cost % = prime cost ÷ sales × 100; each part % = part ÷ sales × 100; remaining = sales − prime cost.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
