# Employee Cost Calculator

**Audience:** small employers, managers and employees checking pay figures.
**Problem:** Headcount decisions get made on salary alone.
**Monetization:** AdSense + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: employerTaxes = salary × rate%; total = salary + employerTaxes + benefits + overheads; perHour = total ÷ productive hours (weeks worked × hours/week × productive %).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, employment-law, tax or benefits advice (rates and rules vary; the tool applies the numbers you enter).
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
