# Gross Profit Margin Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of product and service businesses reading their numbers.
**Problem:** Gross margin is the first number lenders and buyers ask for and it is often confused with markup.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: gross profit = revenue − COGS; margin = gross profit ÷ revenue × 100; markup = gross profit ÷ COGS × 100; revenue for target = COGS ÷ (1 − target margin).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, tax rates by country, tax or accounting advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
