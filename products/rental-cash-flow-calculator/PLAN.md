# Rental Cash Flow Calculator

**Audience:** landlords checking whether a rental pays for itself each month.
**Problem:** Rent minus mortgage looks fine until vacancy and expenses are counted.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Landlord Rent & Expense Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: effective rent = rent × (1 − vacancy %); NOI = effective rent − expenses; cash flow = NOI − mortgage; annual = × 12; expense ratio = expenses ÷ effective rent × 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, market data, mortgage products, tax, investment advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
