# Loan APR Calculator

**Audience:** borrowers comparing offers with different fees and rates.
**Problem:** A lower rate with a higher fee can cost more; APR puts them on one scale.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: payment from the quoted rate; APR = 12 × the monthly rate at which that payment repays (amount − fees) over the term, found by bisection.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, lender-specific terms, credit or financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
