# Procurement Markup Calculator

**Audience:** interior designers, decorators and small design studios who buy furniture, fixtures and finishes for clients.
**Problem:** Markup on cost and margin on price get mixed up, freight eats into procurement profit unseen, and a client can end up paying more than retail without the designer noticing.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: your cost = retail × (1 − trade discount % ÷ 100); client price = entered price, or cost × (1 + markup % ÷ 100) when none is entered; gross profit = client price − cost − freight; margin = gross profit ÷ client price × 100 (0 when price is 0); savings against retail = retail − client price (may be negative). Rejects a trade discount above 100. Page defines procurement and trade discount and explains markup on cost against margin on price and negative savings.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, average markups, usual percentages or ranges, figures from outside the reader's own records, tax, contracts, saving data.
