# Revenue per Employee Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers comparing productivity over time or against a target.
**Problem:** Headcount grows faster than revenue without anyone noticing.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: revenue per employee = revenue ÷ employees; profit per employee = profit ÷ employees; revenue to payroll = revenue ÷ payroll.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, tax rates by country, tax or accounting advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
