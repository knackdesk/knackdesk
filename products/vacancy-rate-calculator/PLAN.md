# Vacancy Rate Calculator

**Audience:** landlords and property managers tracking occupancy.
**Problem:** Vacancy is quoted as a rate but felt as lost rent.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Landlord Rent & Expense Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: vacancy rate = vacant ÷ units × 100; occupancy = 100 − vacancy; lost rent per month = vacant × average rent; per year = × 12.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, market data, mortgage products, tax, investment advice.
