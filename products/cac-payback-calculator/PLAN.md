# CAC Payback Calculator

**Audience:** founders, indie SaaS builders and subscription businesses.
**Problem:** Payback period is the number investors ask for and founders rarely have to hand.
**Monetization:** AdSense (once approved) + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: cac = spend ÷ newCustomers (or entered directly); paybackMonths = cac ÷ (arpa × margin/100).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
