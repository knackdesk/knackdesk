# Call-Out Fee Calculator

**Audience:** plumbers, electricians, locksmiths, repair and IT support businesses that charge a call-out.
**Problem:** Customers ask what a visit will cost; the answer has four parts that are easy to add up wrong.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell (Agency Quote & Project Profit Tracker, Freelance Pricing Kit).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: extra minutes = max(0, minutes on site − included minutes), rounded up to the billing increment; labour = extra minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate; parts price = parts cost × (1 + markup %); total = call-out fee + labour + parts price.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, regional rate tables, tax, contract or legal advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
