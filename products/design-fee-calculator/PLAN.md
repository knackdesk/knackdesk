# Interior Design Fee Calculator

**Audience:** interior designers, decorators and small design studios; also architects and landscape designers who price their own design work.
**Problem:** Design fees are guessed as one round number, so phases, site visits and revision time go unpriced and the client cannot see how the fee is built.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: site visit hours = visits × hours per visit; total hours = concept + development + documentation + site visit hours; base fee = total hours × rate; contingency = base × contingency % ÷ 100; design fee = base + contingency. Rejects an hourly rate of 0 and a total of 0 hours. Page explains the phases and that contingency covers revisions you know will come but cannot count yet.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, average markups, usual percentages or ranges, figures from outside the reader's own records, tax, contracts, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
