# Flat Fee Matter Price Calculator

**Audience:** law firm partners, practice managers and solo practitioners, plus accountants, consultants and other professional practices that quote fixed fees.
**Problem:** Fixed fees quoted from gut feel or bill rates lose money when the matter runs long; pricing from cost with a scope contingency protects the margin.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour cost = Σ hours × cost rate; cost with contingency = labour × (1 + contingency % ÷ 100); total cost = cost with contingency + disbursements; flat fee = total cost ÷ (1 − margin % ÷ 100) (rejects margin of 100 or more); effective hourly rate = flat fee ÷ total hours (null when 0). Page explains that a scope contingency is not a contingency fee.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages or ranges, target percentages, legal advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
