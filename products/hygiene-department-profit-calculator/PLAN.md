# Hygiene Department Profit Calculator

**Audience:** dentists and dental practice owners and managers.
**Problem:** Practices rarely separate hygiene's costs from its production, so they cannot see whether the department pays for itself or what an hour needs to produce to cover its costs.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: loaded wages = wages × (1 + payroll tax % ÷ 100); total cost = loaded wages + supplies + overhead allocation; profit = production − total cost; margin % = profit ÷ production × 100 (0 when production is 0); production per hour = production ÷ hours; cost per hour = total cost ÷ hours (hours of 0 rejected; payroll tax % above 100 rejected). Page explains cost per hour as the break-even production per hour and the overhead allocation as the reader's own choice.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical percentages, averages or ranges, target percentages, clinical advice, treatment pricing advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
