# Photographer Day Rate Calculator

**Audience:** freelance photographers and videographers who book by the day or half day.
**Problem:** Day rates copied from other photographers ignore your own costs, how many days you can actually book and the editing each shoot creates.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: revenue needed = income goal + business costs; day rate = revenue needed ÷ shoot days per year; hours per booking = shoot hours + editing hours; effective hourly = day rate ÷ hours per booking; half-day rate = day rate × 0.6.
**Out of scope:** tax, pension, market rates, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
