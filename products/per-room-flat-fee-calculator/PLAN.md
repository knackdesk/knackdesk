# Per-Room Flat Fee Calculator

**Audience:** interior designers, decorators and small design studios; also architects and landscape designers who price their own design work.
**Problem:** Per-room fees are set by feel, so complex rooms and small projects are underpriced and the minimum fee is applied inconsistently.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: base hours = rooms × hours per room; hours with uplift = base × (1 + uplift % ÷ 100); total hours = uplifted + site visit hours; computed fee = total × rate; flat fee = max(computed, minimum); minimum applied = minimum > computed; fee per room = flat fee ÷ rooms. Rejects 0 rooms and an hourly rate of 0. Page shows a note when the minimum applies.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, average markups, usual percentages or ranges, figures from outside the reader's own records, tax, contracts, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
