# Salary to Hourly Calculator

**Audience:** small employers, managers and employees checking pay figures.
**Problem:** The 2,080-hour shortcut is wrong for most schedules.
**Monetization:** AdSense + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: annual = hourly × hoursPerWeek × weeksPerYear; monthly = annual ÷ 12; weekly = annual ÷ weeksPerYear; daily = weekly ÷ daysPerWeek; hourly = annual ÷ (hoursPerWeek × weeksPerYear).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, employment-law, tax or benefits advice (rates and rules vary; the tool applies the numbers you enter).
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
