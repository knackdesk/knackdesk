# Timekeeper Cost Rate Calculator

**Audience:** law firm partners, practice managers and solo practitioners, plus accountants, consultants and other professional practices that bill time.
**Problem:** Firms set and discount hourly rates without knowing what an hour of each timekeeper costs, so unprofitable work goes unnoticed.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: loaded salary = salary × (1 + benefits % ÷ 100); total annual cost = loaded salary + overhead allocation; cost rate = total annual cost ÷ billable hours (rejects 0 hours); margin per hour = bill rate − cost rate; margin % = margin ÷ bill rate × 100 (0 when bill rate is 0); break-even hours = total annual cost ÷ bill rate (null when bill rate is 0). Page explains that overhead allocation is the firm's own method and changes the result.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages or ranges, target percentages, legal advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
