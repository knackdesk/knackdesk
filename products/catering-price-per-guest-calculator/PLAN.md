# Catering Price per Guest Calculator

**Audience:** caterers, private chefs, event cooks and venues that quote food per head.
**Problem:** Per-head prices built from food cost alone leave out staff, rentals and margin, and margin gets confused with markup.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: food cost = guests × food cost per guest; labour = hours × rate; total cost = food + labour + rentals + other; price = total cost ÷ (1 − margin %); price per guest = price ÷ guests; profit = price − total cost.
**Out of scope:** recipe costing, menu planning, tax, accounts, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
