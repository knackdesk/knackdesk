# Profit per Order Calculator

**Audience:** people selling physical products online through a shop or marketplace.
**Problem:** Sellers see the sale price and miss the five deductions that eat it.
**Monetization:** AdSense + kit cross-sell (fallback to all kits until an ecommerce kit exists).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: fees = price×platformFee% + price×paymentFee% + paymentFixed; profit = price + shippingCharged − cost − packaging − shippingPaid − fees; margin = profit ÷ (price+shippingCharged) × 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, marketplace fee tables (user enters their own rates), tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
