# Matter Budget Variance Calculator

**Audience:** law firm partners, practice managers and solo practitioners, plus accountants, consultants and other professional practices that budget engagements.
**Problem:** Overruns are trimmed off time sheets instead of being traced to scope, estimate or staffing, so the next budget is just as wrong.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: hours and fees variance = actual − budget, as % of budget (0 when budget is 0); realized rate = actual fees ÷ actual hours (null when 0); write-off = max(0, actual fees − agreed fee) when agreed fee > 0, else null; recovery % = agreed fee ÷ actual fees × 100 when both > 0, else null. Page explains positive variance as overrun and what to fix.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages or ranges, target percentages, legal advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
