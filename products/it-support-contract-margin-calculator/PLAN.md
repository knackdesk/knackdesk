# IT Support Contract Margin Calculator

**Audience:** managed service providers (MSPs), IT support companies and freelance IT consultants who want to know whether a support contract pays.
**Problem:** Support contracts drift into loss as usage and licence costs rise; without a margin and break-even hours per contract it goes unnoticed.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour cost = hours × loaded hourly cost; total cost = tools + labour + other; gross profit = fee − total cost; margin % = gross profit ÷ fee × 100 (0 when fee is 0); cost per seat and fee per seat divide by seats (null when 0); break-even hours = (fee − tools − other) ÷ loaded hourly cost (null when cost is 0). Page explains a negative gross profit.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical prices, averages or ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
