# Client Capacity Calculator

**Audience:** bookkeepers, accountants, tax preparers, law firms, consultants and other practices that serve a roster of ongoing clients.
**Problem:** Taking on clients without knowing capacity leads to overbooking; the ceiling is set by hours left after admin and the hours each client takes.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: billable hours = hours × (1 − admin % ÷ 100); maximum clients = floor(billable hours ÷ hours per client); spare hours = billable hours − current clients × hours per client (negative means overbooked); open slots = maximum − current; revenue at capacity = maximum × average fee; current revenue = current × average fee.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
