# Caregiver Utilization Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of home care, domiciliary care, private-duty and companion care agencies, and independent caregivers who run their own books.
**Problem:** Paid travel, training and rota gaps are invisible in billing reports, so agencies cannot see how much paid time never reaches an invoice or where it goes.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: utilization % = billable ÷ paid × 100; non-billable hours = paid − billable; other non-billable = non-billable − travel − training; non-billable cost = non-billable × pay rate; travel share % = travel ÷ paid × 100; training share % = training ÷ paid × 100. Rejects paid hours of 0, billable above paid, and travel plus training above the non-billable hours. Page explains that 100% is not a target because travel and training are part of the job.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, clinical or care advice, tax, saving data.
