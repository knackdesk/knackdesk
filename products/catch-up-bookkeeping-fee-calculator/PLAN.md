# Catch-Up Bookkeeping Fee Calculator

**Audience:** bookkeepers, accountants and tax preparers quoting clean-up work for clients whose books are behind.
**Problem:** Catch-up jobs are hard to estimate and easy to underquote; the quote needs to come from months, hours and rate rather than a round number.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: total hours = months behind × hours per month; labour cost = total hours × hourly rate; subtotal = labour cost + setup fee; discount = subtotal × discount % ÷ 100; total = subtotal − discount; per month = total ÷ months behind.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
