# Labor Cost Percentage Calculator

**Audience:** restaurant, cafe, retail and hospitality managers writing a rota.
**Problem:** A rota written around who is available can quietly cost more of sales than the business planned for.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour cost = hours × wage × (1 + payroll tax % ÷ 100); labour % = labour cost ÷ sales × 100; allowed labour cost = sales × target % ÷ 100; hours allowed = allowed labour cost ÷ (wage × (1 + payroll tax % ÷ 100)); hours over = max(0, hours − hours allowed).
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
