# Cost per Unit Calculator

**Audience:** candle, soap, bakery, cosmetics and small-batch food makers.
**Problem:** Batch costs are known; the per-unit figure that pricing needs is not.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Online Seller Profit Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: batch cost = ingredients + packaging + labour hours × rate + other; usable units = units × (1 − waste%); cost per unit = batch cost ÷ usable units.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, live fee tables (rates are editable defaults), tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
