# Churn Rate Calculator

**Audience:** founders, indie SaaS builders and subscription businesses.
**Problem:** Churn gets quoted without saying whether it is customers or revenue, monthly or yearly.
**Monetization:** AdSense (once approved) + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: customerChurn = lost ÷ startCustomers × 100; revenueChurn = revenueLost ÷ startMRR × 100; retention = 100 − churn; annualised = (1 − (1 − churn/100)^12) × 100 for monthly input.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
