# Table Turnover Calculator

**Audience:** restaurant and cafe owners planning a room, a service or a menu change.
**Problem:** Seat count alone says little about capacity; dining time and how full the room gets decide how many guests a service can take.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: turns per seat = hours open × 60 ÷ average dining minutes; covers per day = seats × turns × occupancy % ÷ 100; revenue per day = covers × average check.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
