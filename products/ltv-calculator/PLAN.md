# Customer Lifetime Value Calculator

**Audience:** founders, indie SaaS builders and subscription businesses.
**Problem:** LTV is quoted with wildly different formulas; this one is explicit.
**Monetization:** AdSense (once approved) + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: lifetimeMonths = 1 ÷ (churn/100); ltv = arpa × (margin/100) × lifetimeMonths; ltvToCac = ltv ÷ cac when cac given.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
