# Utilization Rate Calculator

**Audience:** agencies, consultancies and freelancers who track billable time.
**Problem:** Utilization is quoted everywhere and calculated inconsistently.
**Monetization:** AdSense + category kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: utilization = billable ÷ available × 100; non-billable = available − billable; revenue = billable × rate; hours to target = max(0, target% × available − billable).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, time tracking, tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
