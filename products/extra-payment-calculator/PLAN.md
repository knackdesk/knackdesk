# Extra Payment Calculator

**Audience:** borrowers deciding whether overpaying a loan is worth it.
**Problem:** Overpayments feel small; the interest they save is not.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: scheduled payment from the amortisation formula; the balance is then simulated month by month with the extra payment until it reaches zero; interest saved = scheduled total interest − simulated interest.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, lender-specific terms, credit or financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
