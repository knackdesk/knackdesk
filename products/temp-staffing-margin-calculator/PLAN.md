# Temp Staffing Margin Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of recruitment and staffing agencies, and independent recruiters who supply temporary or contract workers.
**Problem:** Markups are quoted on pay while on-costs (employer taxes, insurance and statutory costs) eat into the bill rate; without a margin on the bill rate, thin assignments look healthy.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: bill rate used = bill rate if above 0, else pay × (1 + markup % ÷ 100); loaded cost = pay × (1 + on-costs % ÷ 100); margin per hour = bill − loaded cost; margin % = margin ÷ bill × 100 (0 when bill is 0); bill per week = bill × hours; margin per week = margin × hours; margin for assignment = margin per week × weeks. Rejects a pay rate of 0. Page explains markup is on pay and margin is on bill rate, and explains a negative margin.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
