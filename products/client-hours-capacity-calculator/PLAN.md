# Client Hours Capacity Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of home care, domiciliary care, private-duty and companion care agencies, and independent caregivers who run their own books.
**Problem:** Agencies accept new care packages without checking whether the rota can staff them, which leads to unstaffed shifts and overtime.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: paid capacity = caregivers × hours; billable capacity = paid × utilization % ÷ 100; maximum clients = floor(billable ÷ client hours); hours committed = current clients × client hours; spare hours = billable − committed; open slots = maximum − current; weekly revenue ceiling = billable × bill rate; current weekly revenue = committed × bill rate. Rejects 0 caregivers, 0 client hours and utilization above 100. Page explains negative spare hours as overcommitted.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, clinical or care advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
