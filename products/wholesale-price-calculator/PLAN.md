# Wholesale Price Calculator

**Audience:** makers and small brands selling to shops as well as direct.
**Problem:** Wholesale and retail margins get confused, and a product that works direct can lose money wholesale.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Online Seller Profit Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: wholesale = cost ÷ (1 − wholesale margin%); retail = wholesale × retail multiplier; from retail: wholesale = retail ÷ multiplier; margin = (wholesale − cost) ÷ wholesale × 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, live fee tables (rates are editable defaults), tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
