# Licensing Fee Calculator

**Audience:** photographers, illustrators and other creatives who license images for commercial use.
**Problem:** Usage fees are hard to explain and to keep consistent from quote to quote without a written method.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: fee = base fee × duration factor × territory factor × media factor × (exclusivity factor if exclusive, otherwise 1); uplift = fee − base fee.
**Out of scope:** industry rate tables, contract wording, legal advice, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
