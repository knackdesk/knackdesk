# Employee Turnover Cost Calculator

**Audience:** small employers, managers and employees checking pay figures.
**Problem:** Turnover is treated as free because its costs are scattered.
**Monetization:** AdSense + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: vacancyCost = dailyCost × vacantDays; rampCost = dailyCost × rampDays × (1 − rampProductivity%); perLeaver = recruiting + vacancyCost + onboardingCost + rampCost; perYear = perLeaver × headcount × turnoverRate%; dailyCost = annualCost ÷ workingDays.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, employment-law, tax or benefits advice (rates and rules vary; the tool applies the numbers you enter).
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
