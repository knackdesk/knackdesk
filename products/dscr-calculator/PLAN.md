# DSCR Calculator

**Audience:** owners and property investors preparing for a loan application.
**Problem:** Lenders decide on DSCR before they look at anything else.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: DSCR = net operating income ÷ annual debt service; max debt service = net operating income ÷ target ratio; monthly = ÷ 12.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, lender-specific terms, credit or financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
