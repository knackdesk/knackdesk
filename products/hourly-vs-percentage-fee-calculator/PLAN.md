# Hourly vs Percentage Fee Calculator

**Audience:** interior designers, decorators and small design studios; also architects and landscape designers who price their own design work.
**Problem:** Designers choose between a percentage-of-budget fee and hourly billing without seeing what each pays on the project in front of them or where the two cross.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: percentage fee = budget × % ÷ 100; hourly fee = hours × rate; difference = percentage − hourly; implied hourly rate = percentage fee ÷ hours (null when 0); hourly fee at overrun = hours × (1 + overrun % ÷ 100) × rate; break-even hours = percentage fee ÷ rate (null when rate is 0). Rejects a percentage fee above 100. Page explains that a percentage fee rises with the budget while the hours may not, and that above the break-even hours the hourly model pays more.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, average markups, usual percentages or ranges, figures from outside the reader's own records, tax, contracts, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
