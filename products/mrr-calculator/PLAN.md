# MRR and ARR Calculator

**Audience:** founders, indie SaaS builders and subscription businesses.
**Problem:** Mixed monthly and annual plans make MRR hard to add up by hand.
**Monetization:** AdSense (once approved) + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: mrr = sum(price × customers ÷ monthsInPeriod); arr = mrr × 12; arpa = mrr ÷ totalCustomers; projection: mrr × (1+g/100)^n.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
