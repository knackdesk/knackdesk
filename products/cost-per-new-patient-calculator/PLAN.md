# Cost per New Patient Calculator

**Audience:** dentists and dental practice owners and managers, and owners of physiotherapy, veterinary, chiropractic, optometry and other clinics.
**Problem:** Practices spend on marketing without knowing what each new patient costs or how that cost compares with what the patient brings in over the years they stay.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: cost per new patient = spend ÷ new patients (0 rejected); lifetime value = first-year value + first-year value × retention % ÷ 100 × (years − 1); value-to-cost ratio = lifetime value ÷ cost (null when cost is 0); net value = lifetime value − cost; break-even new patients = spend ÷ first-year value (null when first-year value is 0). Retention above 100 and years below 1 are rejected. Page explains first-year value as the reader's own figure from their software, not a benchmark.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical percentages, averages or ranges, target percentages, clinical advice, treatment pricing advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
