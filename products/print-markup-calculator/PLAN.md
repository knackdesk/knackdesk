# Print Markup Calculator

**Audience:** photographers and artists who sell prints made by a lab.
**Problem:** Markup and margin get mixed up, so print prices end up thinner than intended once shipping is counted.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: cost = lab cost + shipping; price = cost × (1 + markup %); profit = price − cost; margin = profit ÷ price × 100; price for a target margin = cost ÷ (1 − margin %).
**Out of scope:** lab price lists, framing design, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
