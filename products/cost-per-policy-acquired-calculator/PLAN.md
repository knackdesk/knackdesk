# Cost per Policy Acquired Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of independent insurance agencies and brokerages, and individual producers.
**Problem:** Agencies spend on marketing, leads and producer time without knowing what each new policy costs or how that compares with the commission it earns over the years it stays.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: total cost = marketing + leads + hours × cost per hour; cost per policy = total ÷ policies written (0 rejected); lifetime commission = first-year + renewal × Σ n=1..(years − 1) of (retention % ÷ 100)^n; value-to-cost ratio = lifetime ÷ cost per policy (null when cost is 0); payback policies = total ÷ first-year commission (null when first-year is 0). Retention above 100 and years below 1 or not whole are rejected. Page explains producer hours as the reader's own time cost and lifetime commission as commission, not premium.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical retention rates, typical commission percentages, valuation multiples, averages or ranges, licensing, carrier appointments, compliance, advice on what to sell, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
