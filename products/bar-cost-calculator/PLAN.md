# Bar Cost Calculator

**Audience:** event hosts, caterers, mobile bars and venues estimating a bar.
**Problem:** Guessing drink quantities leads to running dry or paying for stock nobody drinks; the cost per guest is rarely worked out.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: drinks = guests × (first-hour drinks + later-hour drinks × (hours − 1)); drinks by type = drinks × share; cost by type = drinks × cost per drink; cost per guest = total ÷ guests; bartenders = ceil(guests ÷ guests per bartender).
**Out of scope:** bottle and keg conversion, licensing, responsible service advice, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
