# Placement Fee Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of recruitment and staffing agencies, and independent recruiters who want to see what one permanent placement leaves them.
**Problem:** A placement fee looks large on the invoice, but splits, recruiter time and advertising come out of it; without a margin per placement, slow and costly searches go unnoticed.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: gross fee = salary × fee % ÷ 100; split fee = gross fee × split % ÷ 100; net fee = gross fee − split fee; delivery cost = recruiter hours × cost per hour + advertising; margin = net fee − delivery cost; margin % = margin ÷ net fee × 100 (0 when net fee is 0); effective hourly rate = net fee ÷ recruiter hours (null when 0). Rejects fee or split above 100. Page explains cost per hour comes from the recruiter desk cost calculator and that this is one placement, not the desk.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
