# Day Rate to Salary Calculator

**Audience:** freelancers, contractors and small business owners.
**Problem:** Contractors and hiring managers compare day rates and salaries as if they were the same thing.
**Monetization:** AdSense (once approved) + affiliate link where relevant in the explainer.
**Scope:** one client-side calculator (public/index.html + public/calc.js), pure functions unit-tested in test/calc.test.js, 600+ word explainer with FAQ, related-tools block.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, tax or legal advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders in the site build, copy proofread, LAUNCH.md ticked.
