# Gross Rent Multiplier Calculator

**Audience:** investors screening listings quickly before a full analysis.
**Problem:** GRM is the fastest screen there is, as long as everyone uses the same rent figure.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Landlord Rent & Expense Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: GRM = price ÷ annual gross rent; rent to price = monthly rent ÷ price × 100; price for a target GRM = annual rent × target.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, market data, mortgage products, tax, investment advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
