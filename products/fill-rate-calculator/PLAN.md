# Fill Rate Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of recruitment and staffing agencies, and independent recruiters who want to see their own funnel in numbers.
**Problem:** Fill rate, time to fill and conversion sit in the applicant tracking system as raw counts; without the ratios it is hard to see where candidates and orders are lost.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: fill rate % = filled ÷ received × 100; unfilled = received − filled; days to fill per fill = total days ÷ filled (null when 0); submittals per fill = submitted ÷ filled (null when 0); submittal to interview % = interviews ÷ submitted × 100 (null when 0); interview to fill % = filled ÷ interviews × 100 (null when 0). Rejects received of 0 and filled above received. Page sets no target.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
