# Caregiver Turnover Cost Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of home care, domiciliary care, private-duty and companion care agencies, and independent caregivers who run their own books.
**Problem:** Turnover is felt as rota pressure but rarely costed, so spending to keep caregivers is hard to weigh against the cost of replacing them.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: turnover % = leavers ÷ caregivers × 100; cost per leaver = recruiting + onboarding hours × trainer cost + unfilled hours × margin per billable hour; annual cost = cost per leaver × leavers; cost per caregiver = annual ÷ caregivers; saving if halved = annual ÷ 2. Rejects 0 caregivers. Page explains margin per billable hour comes from the bill rate calculator and unfilled hours are shifts lost or covered at overtime.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, clinical or care advice, tax, saving data.
