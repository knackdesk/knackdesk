# Profit Split Calculator

**Audience:** business partners, co-founders and collaborators dividing a profit or a project fee.
**Problem:** Splits are agreed in percentages and argued over in money.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: reserve = profit × reserve %; distributable = profit − reserve; payout = distributable × share %; shares must total 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, tax rates by country, tax or accounting advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
