# Food Cost Percentage Calculator

**Audience:** restaurant, cafe, food truck and catering owners and chefs pricing a menu.
**Problem:** Menu prices set by feel or copied from competitors do not show how much of each sale the ingredients eat.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: food cost % = ingredient cost ÷ menu price × 100; gross profit = menu price − ingredient cost; gross margin % = gross profit ÷ menu price × 100; price for a target = ingredient cost ÷ (target % ÷ 100).
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
