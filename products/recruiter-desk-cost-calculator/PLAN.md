# Recruiter Desk Cost Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of recruitment and staffing agencies, and independent recruiters who want to know what a recruiter desk really costs.
**Problem:** Desk cost is spread across payroll, subscriptions and overhead, so the cost per placement and the break-even number of placements are rarely visible.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: loaded salary = salary × (1 + benefits % ÷ 100); total desk cost = loaded salary + tools + overhead; cost per placement = total ÷ placements; revenue = placements × net fee; desk profit = revenue − total; break-even placements = total ÷ net fee (null when fee is 0); revenue per cost unit = revenue ÷ total (0 when total is 0). Rejects placements of 0. Page explains cost per hour for the placement fee calculator = total desk cost ÷ the reader's own working hours per year.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
