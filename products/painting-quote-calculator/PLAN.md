# Painting Quote Calculator

**Audience:** painters and decorators pricing interior or exterior work.
**Problem:** Paint is priced per tin and labour per hour; the client wants one number.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell (Agency Quote & Project Profit Tracker, Freelance Pricing Kit).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: painted area = area × coats; units of paint = painted area ÷ coverage, rounded up; paint cost = units × price; hours = prep + painted area ÷ area per hour; labour = hours × rate; quote = (paint + labour) × (1 + markup %).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, regional rate tables, tax, contract or legal advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
