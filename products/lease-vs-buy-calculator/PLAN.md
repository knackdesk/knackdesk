# Lease vs Buy Calculator

**Audience:** businesses deciding how to acquire a vehicle, machine or equipment.
**Problem:** Lease and loan quotes are not comparable until both are totals over the same period.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: lease total = payments × months + buyout − resale (resale counts only if you buy it out); buy total = deposit + loan payment × months − resale; loan payment uses the standard amortisation formula.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, lender-specific terms, credit or financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
