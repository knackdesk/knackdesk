# Agent Commission Split Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of independent insurance agencies and brokerages, and individual producers.
**Problem:** Producers and agency owners need to see how a carrier's commission divides between agent and agency once per-policy fees are taken off.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: gross = premium × carrier % ÷ 100 × policies; agent share = gross × split % ÷ 100; agency share = gross − agent share; fees = fee × policies; agent net = agent share − fees; effective % = agent net ÷ (premium × policies) × 100 (0 when premium is 0). Percentages above 100 and policies of 0 are rejected. Page shows the producer's and agency's views and notes a negative agent net.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical retention rates, typical commission percentages, valuation multiples, averages or ranges, licensing, carrier appointments, compliance, advice on what to sell, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
