# Break-Even ROAS Calculator

**Audience:** people selling physical products online through a shop or marketplace.
**Problem:** Campaigns look profitable at 2x ROAS when the real break-even is 3x.
**Monetization:** AdSense + kit cross-sell (fallback to all kits until an ecommerce kit exists).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: breakEvenRoas = 1 ÷ (contributionMargin/100); targetRoas = 1 ÷ ((contributionMargin − targetProfit)/100); contribution margin = price − cost − fees as % of price (page helps compute it).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, marketplace fee tables (user enters their own rates), tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
