# Production per Hour Calculator

**Audience:** dentists and dental practice owners and managers, and owners of physiotherapy, veterinary, chiropractic, optometry and other clinics.
**Problem:** Gross production hides write-downs and the hours it took, so owners cannot tell what an hour of provider time is worth or whether it covers overhead.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: net production = gross − adjustments; production per hour = net ÷ hours (hours of 0 rejected); production per day = net ÷ days (null when days is 0); profit per hour = production per hour − overhead per hour. Adjustments greater than gross production are rejected. Page explains adjustments and that overhead per hour is practice overhead divided by provider hours.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical percentages, averages or ranges, target percentages, clinical advice, treatment pricing advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
