# Policy Retention Rate Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of independent insurance agencies and brokerages, and individual producers.
**Problem:** Agencies know they lose policies each year but not what share of the book that is, how it compares with new business written, or what the losses cost in commission.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: retention % = (start − lost) ÷ start × 100; lapse % = 100 − retention; policies at end = start − lost + new; net growth % = (end − start) ÷ start × 100; commission lost = lost × commission per policy; commission retained = (start − lost) × commission per policy. Policies at start of 0 and policies lost above policies at start are rejected. Page explains policy count vs premium retention and measuring over the same period each time.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical retention rates, typical commission percentages, valuation multiples, averages or ranges, licensing, carrier appointments, compliance, advice on what to sell, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
