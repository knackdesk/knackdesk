# Bookkeeping Package Price Calculator

**Audience:** bookkeepers, accountants and other practices that price ongoing monthly bookkeeping work.
**Problem:** Package prices set by feel drift away from the time the work takes; volume, accounts and extras need to be priced explicitly and checked against hours.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: monthly fee = base fee + transactions × rate per transaction + accounts × fee per account + add-ons; annual fee = monthly fee × 12; effective hourly rate = monthly fee ÷ estimated hours (blank when hours is 0).
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
