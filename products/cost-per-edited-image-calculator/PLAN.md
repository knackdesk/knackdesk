# Cost per Edited Image Calculator

**Audience:** photographers who deliver edited galleries and sell extra images.
**Problem:** Extra image prices are guessed, so they either undercut the package or put clients off.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: total hours = shoot hours + editing hours; total cost = total hours × hourly rate; cost per image = total cost ÷ images delivered; extra image price = cost per image × (1 + markup %); minutes per image = total hours × 60 ÷ images delivered.
**Out of scope:** retouching time per image type, tax, market rates, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
