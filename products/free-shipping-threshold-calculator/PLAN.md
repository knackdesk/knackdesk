# Free Shipping Threshold Calculator

**Audience:** people selling physical products online through a shop or marketplace.
**Problem:** Thresholds get set by copying competitors instead of the maths.
**Monetization:** AdSense + kit cross-sell (fallback to all kits until an ecommerce kit exists).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: breakEvenThreshold = shippingCost ÷ (margin/100); extraProfitAtThreshold = threshold×margin/100 − shippingCost; uplift needed = threshold − AOV.
