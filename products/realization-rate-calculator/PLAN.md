# Realization Rate Calculator

**Audience:** accountants, bookkeepers, tax preparers, law firms, consultants and other practices that record time against a standard rate.
**Problem:** Recorded time is not revenue; time is lost at billing and again at collection, and the practice needs to see where.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: standard fees = hours × standard rate; billing realization = billed ÷ standard fees × 100; collection realization = collected ÷ billed × 100 (0 when nothing billed); overall realization = collected ÷ standard fees × 100; write-offs = standard fees − billed; uncollected = billed − collected; effective rate = collected ÷ hours.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
