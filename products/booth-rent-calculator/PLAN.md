# Booth Rent Calculator

**Audience:** independent stylists, barbers, nail and beauty technicians, massage therapists, personal trainers and anyone who rents space to see clients by appointment.
**Problem:** Rent is fixed while bookings move, so renters rarely know what share of takings the chair costs or how many clients a week simply pay the rent.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: weekly revenue = clients × average ticket; rent % = rent ÷ weekly revenue × 100; net per week = revenue − rent − revenue × product %; net per year = net per week × weeks; annual rent = rent × weeks; break-even clients = rent ÷ (ticket × (1 − product %)), rounded up.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical prices or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
