# Client Onboarding Fee Calculator

**Audience:** managed service providers (MSPs), IT support companies and freelance IT consultants who take on new support clients.
**Problem:** Onboarding work is real cost that goes unrecovered when it is not priced; the choice between an up-front fee and folding it into the monthly fee shifts the risk of early exit.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour = hours × rate; costs with margin = setup costs × (1 + margin % ÷ 100); onboarding fee = labour + costs with margin; per month = fee ÷ contract months (null when 0); contract value = fee + monthly fee × months; fee share = fee ÷ contract value × 100 (0 when contract value is 0). Page explains up-front fee versus folding into the monthly fee.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical prices, averages or ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
