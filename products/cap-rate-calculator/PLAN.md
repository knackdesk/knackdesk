# Cap Rate Calculator

**Audience:** landlords and small investors comparing rental properties.
**Problem:** Cap rate is the first number investors quote and the easiest to get wrong by leaving out vacancy or counting the mortgage.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Landlord Rent & Expense Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: effective income = rent × (1 − vacancy %); NOI = effective income − operating expenses; cap rate = NOI ÷ price × 100; price for a target = NOI ÷ target cap rate.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, market data, mortgage products, tax, investment advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
