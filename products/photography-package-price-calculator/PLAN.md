# Photography Package Price Calculator

**Audience:** wedding, portrait, family and event photographers who sell packages.
**Problem:** Packages priced by feel forget the editing hours and sell albums and prints at or near cost.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour cost = (shoot hours + editing hours) × hourly rate; products price = products cost × (1 + markup %); package price = labour cost + travel + products price; profit on products = products price − products cost.
**Out of scope:** tax, package tier design, market rates, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
