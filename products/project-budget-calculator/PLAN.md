# Project Budget Calculator

**Audience:** freelancers and agencies preparing a quote or internal budget.
**Problem:** Estimates forget expenses and contingency, and the quote is too low before work starts.
**Monetization:** AdSense + category kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour = hours × rate; subtotal = labour + expenses; contingency = subtotal × contingency%; total = (subtotal + contingency) × (1 − discount%); working days = hours ÷ hours per day.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, time tracking, tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
