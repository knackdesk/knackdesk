# Appointment Capacity Calculator

**Audience:** salon owners, stylists, barbers, beauty and massage therapists, personal trainers, tutors and anyone who sells time in appointments.
**Problem:** Opening hours overstate what a week can hold once buffers, part slots and empty bookings are counted, so revenue plans start too high.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: slot minutes = service + buffer; maximum appointments = hours × 60 ÷ slot minutes, rounded down; expected appointments = maximum × utilization % ÷ 100; revenue = appointments × average ticket.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical prices or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
