# Billable Hours Calculator

**Audience:** freelancers and consultants planning the year from an income target.
**Problem:** A revenue goal is easy to set and hard to translate into hours in the calendar.
**Monetization:** AdSense + category kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: hours needed = goal ÷ rate; per week = hours ÷ weeks; per day = per week ÷ days; utilization = per week ÷ available hours × 100; feasible when utilization ≤ 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, time tracking, tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
