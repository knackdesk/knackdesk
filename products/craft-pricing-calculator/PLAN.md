# Craft Pricing Calculator

**Audience:** makers selling handmade goods at markets, on Etsy or to shops.
**Problem:** Makers price by gut feel and forget to pay themselves for time and overhead.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Online Seller Profit Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour = hours × rate; total cost = (materials + labour) × (1 + overhead%); wholesale = cost × wholesale markup; retail = wholesale × retail markup.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, live fee tables (rates are editable defaults), tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
