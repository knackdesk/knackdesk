# Event Budget Calculator

**Audience:** event planners, couples, office managers and small organisers budgeting an event.
**Problem:** Event costs sit in separate quotes; nobody sees the per-guest cost or which line is eating the budget until it is too late.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: total = sum of lines; per guest = total ÷ guests; remaining = budget − total (negative is over budget); share = line ÷ total × 100; line per guest = line ÷ guests.
**Out of scope:** vendor directories, regional price tables, saving data, tax.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
