# Practice Overhead Percentage Calculator

**Audience:** dentists and dental practice owners and managers, and owners of physiotherapy, veterinary, chiropractic, optometry and other clinics.
**Problem:** Owners see collections and bills but not what share of the money goes on running the practice, or whether the practice supports the pay they take out.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: total overhead = staff + rent and facilities + supplies and lab + marketing + other (owner compensation is not overhead); overhead % = overhead ÷ collections × 100 (0 when collections is 0); profit before owner = collections − overhead; profit after owner = profit before owner − owner compensation; profit margin % = profit before owner ÷ collections × 100 (0 when 0). Page explains why owner pay is shown separately and what a negative profit after owner pay means.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical percentages, averages or ranges, target percentages, clinical advice, treatment pricing advice, tax, saving data.
