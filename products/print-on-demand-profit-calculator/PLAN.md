# Print on Demand Profit Calculator

**Audience:** people selling t-shirts, mugs and prints through Printful, Printify, Redbubble-style platforms or their own shop.
**Problem:** Base cost plus shipping plus two fees often leaves a few dollars; sellers find out late.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Online Seller Profit Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: revenue = price + shipping charged; fees = revenue × platform% + revenue × payment% + fixed; profit = revenue − base cost − shipping cost − fees; margin = profit ÷ revenue × 100; sales for target = target ÷ profit, rounded up.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, live fee tables (rates are editable defaults), tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
