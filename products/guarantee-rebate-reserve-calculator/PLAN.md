# Guarantee Rebate Reserve Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of recruitment and staffing agencies, and independent recruiters who offer a rebate or refund guarantee on permanent placements.
**Problem:** Rebates for fall-offs inside the guarantee period arrive without warning and come out of current cash unless a reserve has been set aside.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: expected rebate = fee × rebate % ÷ 100 × fall-off % ÷ 100; net expected fee = fee − expected rebate; reserve % of fee = rebate % × fall-off % ÷ 100; annual reserve = expected rebate × placements; annual net fees = net expected fee × placements; guarantee days echoed. Rejects fall-off or rebate above 100. Page explains fall-off, that the rate is the reader's own history, and that a reserve is money set aside, not a cost on every placement.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
