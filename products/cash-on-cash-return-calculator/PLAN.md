# Cash on Cash Return Calculator

**Audience:** investors buying rentals with a mortgage who want the return on their own money.
**Problem:** Cap rate ignores financing; cash on cash is the return on the cash actually invested.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Landlord Rent & Expense Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: annual cash flow = NOI − annual debt service; cash on cash = annual cash flow ÷ cash invested × 100; monthly cash flow = annual ÷ 12.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, market data, mortgage products, tax, investment advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
