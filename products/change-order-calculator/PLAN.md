# Change Order Calculator

**Audience:** contractors and builders pricing extra work after the contract is signed.
**Problem:** Change orders are small and disruptive, and pricing them at cost gives the margin away.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell (Agency Quote & Project Profit Tracker, Freelance Pricing Kit).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: own cost = materials + hours × rate; change price = own cost × (1 + markup %) + subcontractors × (1 + sub markup %); revised total = original + previous change orders + change price; change % = change price ÷ original × 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, regional rate tables, tax, contract or legal advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
