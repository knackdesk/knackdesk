# Design Project Budget Allocation Calculator

**Audience:** interior designers, decorators and small design studios; also architects and landscape designers who price their own design work.
**Problem:** A client's budget is agreed as one number, and nobody checks whether the design fee, furniture, construction and contingency fit inside it until the money runs out.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: each line = budget × its % ÷ 100 for design fee, furniture, construction and contingency; allocated % = sum of the four percentages; allocated amount = sum of the four amounts; remaining = budget − allocated (negative when over-allocated). Rejects any percentage above 100 and a budget of 0. Page shows an over-allocated note and says the splits are the reader's own plan for this project, not a rule.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees, average markups, usual percentages or ranges, figures from outside the reader's own records, tax, contracts, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
