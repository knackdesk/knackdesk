# Chair Utilization Calculator

**Audience:** dentists and dental practice owners and managers, and owners of physiotherapy, veterinary, chiropractic, optometry and other clinics.
**Problem:** Owners know the schedule feels full or empty but not how many chair hours sit idle each month or how much production those hours could hold.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: available chair hours = chairs × hours per day × days; utilization % = scheduled ÷ available × 100; idle hours = available − scheduled; current production = scheduled × rate; production at full = available × rate; unrealised = full − current. Chairs of 0, available hours of 0 and scheduled hours above available are rejected. Page states that 100% is not a goal and that unrealised production is a ceiling, not a forecast.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical percentages, averages or ranges, target percentages, clinical advice, treatment pricing advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
