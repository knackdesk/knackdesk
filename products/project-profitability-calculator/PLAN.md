# Project Profitability Calculator

**Audience:** agencies and freelancers checking whether a fixed-price job pays.
**Problem:** Fixed prices hide losses until the hours are counted.
**Monetization:** AdSense + category kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: labour cost = hours × cost per hour; total cost = labour + expenses; profit = price − total cost; margin = profit ÷ price × 100; effective rate = price ÷ hours; break-even hours = (price − expenses) ÷ cost per hour.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, time tracking, tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
