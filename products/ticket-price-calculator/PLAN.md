# Ticket Price Calculator

**Audience:** event organisers, promoters, workshop hosts and community groups selling tickets.
**Problem:** Ticket prices set from cost ÷ attendees forget the platform fee and leave no room for profit.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: total cost = fixed + cost per attendee × attendees; break-even price = total cost ÷ (attendees × (1 − fee %)); target price = (total cost + profit) ÷ (attendees × (1 − fee %)); revenue = target price × attendees; fees = revenue × fee %.
**Out of scope:** tiered or dynamic pricing, tax, ticketing platform comparisons, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
