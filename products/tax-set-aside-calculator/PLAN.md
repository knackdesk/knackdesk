# Tax Set-Aside Calculator

**Audience:** freelancers and sole traders who pay tax in lump sums rather than through payroll.
**Problem:** Tax bills arrive long after the income and the money is often gone.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: profit = income − expenses; set-aside = max(0, profit) × percent; converted to per month, per quarter and per year from the period entered.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, tax rates by country, tax or accounting advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
