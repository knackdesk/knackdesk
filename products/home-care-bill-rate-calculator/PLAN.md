# Home Care Bill Rate Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of home care, domiciliary care, private-duty and companion care agencies, and independent caregivers who run their own books.
**Problem:** Bill rates set from pay alone miss on-costs (employer taxes, insurance and statutory costs), paid travel and training that cannot be billed, and overhead, so the rate can sit below break-even without anyone noticing.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: loaded pay rate = pay × (1 + on-costs % ÷ 100); labour cost per billable hour = loaded pay × (1 + non-billable paid % ÷ 100); overhead per billable hour = monthly overhead ÷ billable hours; cost per billable hour (break-even) = labour + overhead; bill rate = cost ÷ (1 − margin % ÷ 100); margin per hour = bill rate − cost. Rejects billable hours of 0 and a margin of 100 or more. Page explains margin on the bill rate versus markup on cost.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, clinical or care advice, tax, saving data.
