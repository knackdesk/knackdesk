# Net Profit Margin Calculator

**Audience:** small business owners checking what is left of each unit of revenue.
**Problem:** Net margin is quoted without saying what was deducted on the way.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: gross = revenue − cost of sales; operating = gross − operating expenses; net = operating − interest − tax; each margin = profit ÷ revenue × 100.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, tax rates by country, tax or accounting advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
