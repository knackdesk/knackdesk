# Recipe Cost Calculator

**Audience:** chefs, bakers, cafe owners and caterers costing a recipe or a batch.
**Problem:** Costing a dish from memory misses small ingredients, trim and waste, so the cost per portion comes out too low.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: raw cost = Σ quantity × unit cost; batch cost = raw cost × (1 + waste % ÷ 100); cost per portion = batch cost ÷ portions; food cost % = cost per portion ÷ menu price × 100.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
