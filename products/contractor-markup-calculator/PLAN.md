# Contractor Markup Calculator

**Audience:** contractors setting a standard markup for quotes.
**Problem:** A 20% markup is not a 20% margin, and a markup that ignores overhead loses money on every job.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell (Agency Quote & Project Profit Tracker, Freelance Pricing Kit).
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: price = cost × (1 + markup); margin = (price − cost) ÷ price; markup for a margin = margin ÷ (1 − margin); markup for overhead and profit = 1 ÷ (1 − (overhead % + profit %)) − 1.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, regional rate tables, tax, contract or legal advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
