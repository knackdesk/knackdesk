# Deposit and Milestone Split Calculator

**Audience:** freelancers and small agencies who invoice clients.
**Problem:** Freelancers do not know how to structure a deposit and milestones for a fixed-price project.
**Monetization:** AdSense (once approved) + affiliate link to an invoicing tool in the explainer; cross-sell to a future paid invoicing kit.
**Scope:** one client-side calculator (public/index.html + public/calc.js), pure functions unit-tested in test/calc.test.js, 600+ word explainer with FAQ on the same page, related-tools block.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, currency conversion, legal advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders in the site build, copy proofread, LAUNCH.md ticked.
