# Contingency Fee Split Calculator

**Audience:** law firm partners, practice managers and solo practitioners who take matters on a contingency basis.
**Problem:** Clients and firms argue over settlement figures because the order of deductions and the referral share are not worked through in money.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: fee base = settlement, or settlement − costs when costs come off first; gross fee = fee base × contingency % ÷ 100 (rejects above 100); referral fee = gross fee × referral % ÷ 100; net fee to firm = gross fee − referral fee; net to client = settlement − gross fee − costs (rejects costs above settlement); effective hourly rate = net fee ÷ hours (null when 0). Page states the order of deductions is set by the agreement and local rules, and that what may be agreed is a matter for the jurisdiction and professional body.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages or ranges, target percentages, legal advice, tax, saving data.
