# Business Loan Calculator

**Audience:** owners comparing loan offers or checking what a loan will cost.
**Problem:** Lenders quote a rate; the cost is the interest over the term.
**Monetization:** AdSense + planning kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n) with r = annual rate ÷ 12; total = payment × n; interest = total − P; max principal = payment × (1 − (1 + r)^−n) ÷ r.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, lender-specific terms, credit or financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
