# Hourly to Fixed Fee Calculator

**Audience:** bookkeepers, accountants, tax preparers and other practices moving ongoing clients from hourly billing to a fixed monthly price.
**Problem:** A fixed fee moves the risk of overruns to the practice; the fee needs a buffer and a test of what a long month does to the hourly rate.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: base monthly = hours × rate; with buffer = base × (1 + buffer % ÷ 100); fixed fee = with buffer × (1 − commitment discount % ÷ 100); annual fee = fixed fee × 12; effective hourly at overrun = fixed fee ÷ (hours × (1 + overrun % ÷ 100)).
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical fees or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
