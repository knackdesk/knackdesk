# Etsy Fee Calculator

**Audience:** sellers on Etsy who want to know what a sale actually leaves them.
**Problem:** Etsy fees stack on both price and shipping and are easy to under-count.
**Monetization:** AdSense + Online Seller Profit Tracker cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: transaction = (price + shipping) × transaction%; payment = (price + shipping) × payment% + fixed; offsite = (price + shipping) × offsite%; profit = price + shipping − fees − item cost − shipping cost.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, live fee tables (rates are editable defaults), tax advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
