# Lock-Up Days Calculator

**Audience:** law firm partners, practice managers and solo practitioners.
**Problem:** Firms wait months to be paid for work already done without seeing whether the delay sits in billing or in collection.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: daily fees = annual fees ÷ days in year (rejects 0 for either); WIP days = unbilled WIP ÷ daily fees; debtor days = receivables ÷ daily fees; lock-up days = WIP days + debtor days; locked-up cash = WIP + receivables; cash per day of lock-up = daily fees. Page explains the two parts and their different fixes.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages or ranges, target percentages, legal advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
