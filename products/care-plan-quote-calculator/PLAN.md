# Care Plan Quote Calculator

**Audience:** owners and managers of home care, domiciliary care, private-duty and companion care agencies, and independent caregivers who run their own books.
**Problem:** Quotes for mixed weekday, weekend and overnight care with mileage are worked out by hand, which leads to errors and an hourly figure the client cannot check.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: standard hours = hours − weekend − overnight; standard cost = standard × bill rate; weekend cost = weekend × bill rate × (1 + uplift % ÷ 100); overnight cost = overnight × overnight rate; mileage = miles × mileage rate; weekly total = sum; period total = weekly × weeks; effective hourly rate = weekly ÷ hours (null when 0). Rejects weekend plus overnight above hours per week. Page says the rates are the reader's own terms and the quote is an estimate, not an invoice.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical rates, averages from outside the reader's own records, ranges, target percentages, clinical or care advice, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
