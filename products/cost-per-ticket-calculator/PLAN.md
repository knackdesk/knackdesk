# Cost per Ticket Calculator

**Audience:** managed service providers (MSPs), IT support companies and freelance IT consultants who run a help desk and price support contracts.
**Problem:** Without a cost per ticket, a client's ticket history cannot be turned into a cost to compare with what they pay.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: total cost = technician cost + tools + overhead; cost per ticket = total ÷ tickets; labour cost per ticket = technician cost ÷ tickets; tickets per technician = tickets ÷ technicians (null when 0); hours on tickets = tickets × minutes ÷ 60. Rejects 0 tickets.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical prices, averages or ranges, target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
