# Net Revenue Retention Calculator

**Audience:** founders, indie SaaS builders and subscription businesses.
**Problem:** NRR and GRR are mixed up constantly in pitch decks.
**Monetization:** AdSense (once approved) + kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: nrr = (start + expansion − contraction − churned) ÷ start × 100; grr = (start − contraction − churned) ÷ start × 100 (expansion excluded, min 0).
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, financial advice.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
