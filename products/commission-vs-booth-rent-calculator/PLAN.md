# Commission vs Booth Rent Calculator

**Audience:** stylists, barbers, nail and beauty technicians, massage therapists and personal trainers choosing between commission and renting their own space.
**Problem:** A commission split and a weekly rent are hard to compare by eye, and the answer flips at a revenue level few people work out.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: booth rent per month = weekly rent × 52 ÷ 12; commission take-home = revenue × commission %; booth take-home = revenue − monthly rent − revenue × product %; difference = booth − commission; break-even revenue = monthly rent ÷ (1 − commission % − product %), none when that is 0 or less.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical prices or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
