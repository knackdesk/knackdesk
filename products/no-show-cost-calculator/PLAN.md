# No-Show Cost Calculator

**Audience:** salon owners, stylists, barbers, beauty and massage therapists, personal trainers, tutors and anyone who sells appointments.
**Problem:** Single no-shows feel small, so their yearly cost and the value of a deposit or no-show fee are rarely worked out.
**Monetization:** AdSense + pricing kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 650+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: no-shows per week = appointments × no-show % ÷ 100; lost per week = no-shows × ticket; lost per year = lost per week × weeks; fee recovered = no-shows × fee × weeks; net lost = lost per year − fee recovered.
**Out of scope:** industry benchmarks, typical prices or target percentages, tax, saving data.
**Done when:** tests pass, page renders, LAUNCH.md ticked.
