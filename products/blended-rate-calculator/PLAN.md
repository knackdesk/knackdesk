# Blended Rate Calculator

**Audience:** agencies and consultancies quoting one rate for work done by several people.
**Problem:** Clients want one number; teams have five rates.
**Monetization:** AdSense + category kit cross-sell.
**Scope:** client-side calculator, pure tested functions, 600+ word explainer with FAQ. Formulas: blended rate = Σ(hours × rate) ÷ Σ hours.
**Out of scope:** accounts, saving data, time tracking, tax advice.
