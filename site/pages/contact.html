Contact

The only way to reach Knackdesk is by email at hello@knackdesk.com. Every message is read. Purchase problems and reported errors are answered first, usually within a few working days.

Reporting a calculation error

If a result looks wrong, tell us:

  • which page you were on (the address is enough);
  • the exact inputs you entered;
  • the result you got;
  • the result you expected, and how you worked it out if you can.

We check the case against the calculator's tests, fix the page if it is wrong and update its reviewed date. The methodology page explains how calculators are tested.

Questions about a workbook

Please say which kit you bought, which app and version you opened it in (for example Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or LibreOffice), and what happened. Purchases are processed and delivered by Polar, so your receipt and download link come from Polar. If a file you bought does not open or does not work as described, we will fix it or arrange a refund through Polar, as set out in the terms.

Suggesting a calculator

Describe the problem you are trying to solve and the figures you already have. Suggestions that come with real inputs are the easiest to turn into a useful tool. Not every suggestion can be built, but each one is kept and read when deciding what to build next.

What we do not do

  • We do not build custom spreadsheets or one-off calculators.
  • We do not give financial, tax or legal advice, and cannot say what rules apply in your jurisdiction.
  • We do not take part in partnerships, sponsored posts or link exchanges.
