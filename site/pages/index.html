Small, useful tools and kits

Free calculators for freelancers, contractors and small businesses. Each one does a single job, such as working out an invoice due date, a late fee, an hourly rate or how long your cash will last. They run in your browser, need no account and send nothing anywhere. Every formula is shown on the page and checked by automated tests. When you want everything in one place, the spreadsheet kits bring the same calculations together in a workbook you buy once and keep.
