About Knackdesk

Knackdesk is a small independent studio, run since 2026 by a software engineer based in Croatia. It makes free, single-purpose calculators for freelancers, contractors and small businesses, and a few spreadsheet kits for the people who want the same calculations in one workbook.

What the tools are

Each tool answers one practical question you meet when running a small business: when an invoice is due, what a late fee comes to, what to charge per hour or per day, the difference between markup and margin, how long your cash will last, how many working days sit between two dates, and similar. Every tool runs entirely in your browser. There is no account to create, and nothing you type is sent to a server or stored.

How they are built and checked

The arithmetic behind every tool lives in a small, separate module that is covered by automated unit tests, including edge cases such as zero values, weekends, rounding and invalid input. Each formula is checked against worked examples before a tool is published, and the formula is written out on the tool's page so you can verify the result yourself. Pages carry a "last reviewed" date showing when the content and formulas were last checked.

Found an error?

If a result looks wrong, or an explanation is unclear, please email hello@knackdesk.com with the tool, the numbers you entered and what you expected. Corrections are made quickly and the page's review date is updated.

Not advice

The tools do arithmetic and explain common conventions. They are not legal, tax or financial advice, and rules differ between countries and contracts. For anything important, check the figures with a qualified adviser.

How the site is funded

The tools are free and will stay free. The site is supported by sales of the spreadsheet kits, which are sold and delivered through Polar, and by advertising on some pages.
